# Roadmap

## Milestone 1: Inventory core

- Product, brand, and category management
- Suppliers and customers
- Goods receiving
- Immutable stock movements and current stock
- Basic sales records
- Basic reports
- Roles and server-side authorization

## Milestone 2: Digital sales layer

- Public product catalog
- Product detail and technical documents
- Quote requests
- WhatsApp handoff
- Request management

## Later, only after validation
- Purchase orders, purchase invoices, and supplier payments
- Unit conversions (meters, coils, pieces) for cable-type products
- Customer-specific B2B pricing and repeat orders
- Mobile workflows
- Windows offline client and synchronization
- Accounting/ERP integration
- Multi-tenancy and white-label configuration
- Advanced CRM and automation
