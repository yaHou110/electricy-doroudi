# Droudian Platform

Droudian Platform is a Persian-first business management system for electrical distributors. The first customer workflow is focused on receiving goods, maintaining reliable stock history, recording sales, and giving staff a clear operational dashboard.

## Users

- Manager: monitors stock, sales, and operational summaries.
- Warehouse staff: receives goods and records stock movements.
- Sales staff: searches products, records sales, and manages customers.

## Product direction

The application is being built for the first customer but should remain reusable. Customer-specific branding and settings belong in configuration; business behavior that is useful to other distributors belongs in the shared product.

## Current delivery

Milestone 1 delivers the inventory core. The public catalog, quote requests, and WhatsApp handoff are planned for milestone 2.
